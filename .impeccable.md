# Design Context

## Users

Two readers, always both. An **agent** reads the skill files and writes a page from them, so the recipes must be copyable and unambiguous. A **person** reads the generated page to check work they are responsible for, and to steer it while it is still in motion.

The person is usually mid-task and context-switching. They came to the page with a question. The page earns its place only if it answers that question faster than the terminal would.

## Brand personality

Precise, plain, unshowy. The page is a working surface, not a showcase. It states the conclusion, attaches the evidence, and gives the reader a handle on any decision that is still open.

Three words: **exact, plain, unhurried.**

## Aesthetic direction

The skill does not have one look. It has a rotation of committed looks, defined in `SKILL.md`: blueprint, editorial, paper/ink, monochrome terminal, engraved ledger, risograph zine, field guide, and named IDE themes. Each page picks one and commits. Variety across pages is the goal, and sameness across pages is the failure.

Anti-references live in `references/anti-patterns.md` and are binding: no Inter, no purple-cyan gradients, no gradient text, no emoji section headers, no accent side-stripes, no glow or pulse.

## Design principles

1. **The page is checkable.** Every claim about a real artifact carries its source. A conclusion without its evidence is the defect.
2. **The page is steerable.** Where a decision is open, the reader can act on it and hand the result back. An untouched field never reads as agreement.
3. **Size encodes truth.** A funnel stage, a pyramid tier, a sector angle: each comes from the real number, and prints that number as text. A shape that only looks like data is decoration.
4. **Contrast is not negotiable.** Ink and fill are separate tones. A pale fill takes dark ink. The floors in `css-patterns.md` hold everywhere.
5. **Structure carries meaning.** Pick the shape whose logic matches the content's logic, then vary structure and item motif across pages, not only the palette.

## Layout shapes: fidelity

The shapes in `references/structures.md` stay **static and print-like** — as does the whole kit. Pages carry no entrance animation, no staggered reveals, no decorative hover; they render complete and at rest. A shape gains interaction styling only when it becomes genuinely interactive, and then it gains a focus state and a real hit area, never decorative hover.
